### Astro: the standalone opt-out confirmation on every island, and the side Drawer loads at once

- **`<OptOutConfirmation />` shows its confirmation with `ui: 'react'` and `ui: 'vue'`.** Before, the React and Vue dialog islands opened the preference dialog on its receipt instead, even on a page that placed `<OptOutConfirmation />`. The Svelte island already showed the standalone confirmation. A page without `<OptOutConfirmation />` still confirms an opt-out on the dialog's receipt.
- **A prompt shown as a side Drawer loads the consent engine at once.** The Drawer on the start or end side of a desktop screen shows every choice from the first paint, so the engine and the Drawer's panel no longer wait for the page's priority images or the visitor's first input. A prompt shown as a Card or Banner still waits, as before.
