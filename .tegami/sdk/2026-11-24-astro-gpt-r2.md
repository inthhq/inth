### Astro: a tap on a banner restored after a failed load retries the load

- When the consent prompt's chunk fails to load after a visitor tapped the server-rendered banner, the banner shows again. A tap on it now loads the chunk again, and the choice commits once the prompt mounts. Before, nothing loaded it again until a `ClientRouter` navigation, so the tap went nowhere.
- On a blocking banner, a tap on the banner restored after the consent engine failed to load now loads the engine again, as it already did on a non-blocking banner.
