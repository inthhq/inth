### Upgrade c15t apps to v3 with `inth c15t codemods`

`inth c15t codemods` runs the c15t v2 to v3 codemods. Name the codemods to run, and add `--dry-run` to preview the changes first. `--list` prints them in the order they run. It needs no sign-in or Inth project.

Its output matches `c15t codemods` from `@c15t/cli@3.1.0`, with one exception: offline mode stays as it is, with a `TODO(c15t v3)` comment to finish by hand.
