<section id="behavior">
  ## What ConsentDialogTrigger renders

  `ConsentDialogTrigger` renders a draggable floating button fixed to a corner
  of the viewport that opens the preference dialog. It takes no props and
  reads its configuration from your Inth options.

  The trigger mounts in `document.body` in the browser only. It stays hidden
  while the preference dialog is open, before the visitor's policy resolves,
  and when the policy requires no consent UI. With the default
  `triggerShowWhen: 'after-consent'`, it also hides while the banner awaits an
  answer and appears once the visitor saves a choice or dismisses a notice.

  While `ConsentDevTools` is mounted, the trigger expands into a two-button
  toolbar pairing preferences with a DevTools button that opens the panel next
  to the toolbar.
</section>

<section id="options">
  ## Options

  | Option | Type | Default | Behavior |
  | --- | --- | --- | --- |
  | `showTrigger` | `boolean` | `false` | Renders the trigger from `InthProvider`. |
  | `triggerDefaultPosition` | `'top-left' \| 'top-right' \| 'bottom-left' \| 'bottom-right'` | `'bottom-right'` | Starting viewport corner. |
  | `triggerSize` | `'sm' \| 'md' \| 'lg'` | `'md'` | Button size: 32, 40, or 48 pixels. |
  | `triggerIcon` | `'branding' \| 'fingerprint' \| 'settings'` | `'fingerprint'` | Icon inside the button. `'branding'` shows the Inth mark. |
  | `triggerPersistPosition` | `boolean` | `true` | Saves dragged position in localStorage under `inth:dialog-trigger-position`. |
  | `triggerShowWhen` | `'always' \| 'after-consent' \| 'never'` | `'after-consent'` | `'after-consent'` shows the trigger after a choice or notice dismissal; `'always'` shows it while the banner is open; `'never'` hides it. |
  | `triggerAriaLabel` | `string` | `'Open privacy settings'` | Accessible button label. |
</section>

<section id="accessibility">
  ## Accessibility and styling

  The trigger is a native `button` with `aria-label` set from
  `triggerAriaLabel` and its icon hidden from assistive technology. Set
  `triggerAriaLabel` in the visitor's language when your site supports
  multiple languages.

  `components.trigger` binds attributes to `root`, `icon`, and `text`. Setting
  `components.trigger.text`, even to `{}`, shows `triggerAriaLabel` as visible
  text beside the icon. The button carries `data-size` and, while dragged,
  `data-dragging`. When `ConsentDevTools` is mounted, style the toolbar with
  `toolbar`, `toolbarItem`, and `toolbarIcon` instead.
</section>

<section id="verify">
  ## Verify

  Load a page under a policy with a prompt and answer the banner. Confirm the
  button appears in the configured corner, opens preferences when clicked, and
  hides while the dialog is open. Drag it, reload, and confirm it keeps its
  position when `triggerPersistPosition` is enabled.
</section>
