<section id="behavior">
  ## What ConsentManager renders

  `ConsentManager` is the consent dialog, "Your Privacy Choices"
  (equivalent to `ConsentDialog` in React). It opens while `activeUI` is
  `'dialog'`, set by `PrivacyChoicesLink`, `ConsentDialogLink`,
  `ConsentDialogTrigger`, a `ConsentGate` button, or your own code. The
  banner's Customize button does not open it: Customize opens the choices
  inside the banner. It renders nothing until the visitor's policy resolves
  or when the policy requires no consent UI.

  The dialog is centred on desktop and a bottom sheet on phones. Its content
  follows the policy regime:

  - **Choice regime** (opt-in, and opt-out rules that ask for a choice): one
    switch per category in scope, with its vendors, and Reject All, Accept
    All, and Save. Save stays disabled until a switch differs from the
    recorded choice. Necessary stays on.
  - **Opt-out regime** (opt-out rules with a notice): the sale-and-sharing
    row with Protect and Allow, the receipt of the recorded choice, a
    Preferences row for the category switches, and Done. Allowing again
    after an opt-out asks to confirm first (11 CCR §7028(a)).

  An opt-out recorded in one click, such as from a "Do Not Sell" link or
  after a `reloadOnConsentRevoked` reload, opens the dialog with focus on its
  receipt. A standalone `<OptOutConfirmation />` you mounted shows the
  confirmation instead. The dialog hosts the opt-in confirmation while it is
  mounted.

  The dialog's code and stylesheets load in a chunk of their own when it
  first opens, or earlier when the visitor reaches for a banner button that
  can open it.

  Escape and Close close the dialog without saving. If the policy still owes
  a choice, the banner returns. Reject All, Accept All, and Save close the
  dialog as soon as the browser records the choice. Clicking the backdrop
  does not close it. Closing discards unsaved switches, so the next open
  starts from the recorded choice.

  While Global Privacy Control is active under a policy that maps it, the
  signal-restricted categories stay off and their switches are disabled with
  the GPC note. Read the same state and receipt with `useOptOutState()`.

  Copy comes from the `privacyChoices` translation section and
  `consentTypes.<category>.title` and `.description` for each category.
  `dialogLegalLinks` selects which `legalLinks` appear in the footer. The
  dialog takes one prop, `disableAnimation`, and no slots or text props.
</section>

<section id="accessibility">
  ## Accessibility and focus

  The dialog renders in `document.body` with `role="dialog"`,
  `aria-modal="true"`, and `aria-labelledby` pointing at its title. It always
  blocks the page: backdrop, scroll lock, and focus trap. Opening moves focus
  to the title, Tab and Shift+Tab wrap inside it, and closing returns focus
  to the element that opened it, or to "Your Privacy Choices" when that
  element is gone. Its `dir` attribute matches the active language. Status
  messages, such as the saved cue, use a `role="status"` region.

  Each category switch uses the category title as its accessible name. On
  phones, the Preferences row and each category's vendor list open as a
  nested sheet with its own title and close button.
</section>

<section id="styling">
  ## Style the dialog

  Style the dialog with [theme tokens](/docs/customization/tokens) and these
  `components` parts: `dialog.root`, `dialog.container`, `dialog.card`,
  `dialog.header`, `dialog.title`, `dialog.content`, `dialog.footer`,
  `dialog.sheet`, `dialog.categoryGroup`, `dialog.vendorTable`,
  `dialog.rightsRow`, `dialog.receipt`, `dialog.preferencesRow`,
  `dialog.saveButton`, `dialog.doneButton`, and `description.dialog`.
  `dialog.overlay` styles the backdrop, which is the dialog's root element.
  A part with `noStyle: true` drops its stock classes. See
  [component parts](/docs/customization/slots).

  `disableAnimation` turns off transitions.
</section>

<section id="verify">
  ## Verify

  Open the dialog from your "Your Privacy Choices" link, toggle one optional
  category on, and click Save. Reload and reopen the dialog to confirm the
  switch stays on. Press Escape while open and confirm the dialog closes and
  returns focus to the link that opened it.
</section>
