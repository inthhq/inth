<section id="behavior">
  ## What ConsentDialogLink renders

  `ConsentDialogLink` renders a `button` that sets `activeUI` to `'manager'`
  to open the preference dialog. With `right="opt-out"` under an opt-out
  policy, the action follows the resolved `presentation.optOutLink` title:
  `'do-not-sell'` (the default) records an opt-out in one click and opens
  `OptOutConfirmation`, while `'privacy-choices'` and
  `'california-privacy-choices'` render with the California Privacy Protection
  Agency opt-out icon and open the preference dialog without recording an
  opt-out. Programmatic calls to `useOptOut()` still opt out in one step
  whatever `presentation.optOutLink` is set to. Under other policy models the
  link opens the preference dialog. When `right` is omitted, the default label
  comes from the `consentManagerDialog.title` translation ("Privacy Settings" in
  English), updating to the visitor's language once init delivers copy. Pass
  default slot content to replace the label.

  <include src="../../privacy-choices-link.mdx" />

  The button renders only when preferences apply:

  - It stays hidden until the visitor's policy resolves.
  - It stays hidden when the policy requires no consent UI (no prompt and no
    rights).

  Opening the dialog requires `ConsentManager` to be mounted, which
  `InthProvider` handles automatically.
</section>

<section id="accessibility">
  ## Accessibility and styling

  The component renders a native `button` with `type="button"`, supporting
  Enter and Space without link semantics. Attributes such as `class` fall
  through to the button. It carries `data-testid="consent-dialog-link"` and
  `data-inth-rights` containing the policy's space-separated rights.
</section>

<section id="verify">
  ## Verify

  Load the page under a policy that asks for consent and click **Privacy
  Settings** to confirm the preference dialog opens. Under a policy with no
  prompt and no rights, confirm the button is absent from the DOM.
</section>
