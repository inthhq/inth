<section id="behavior">
  ## What ConsentWidget renders

  `ConsentWidget` renders the preference controls inline in page flow for a
  privacy or account settings page. It displays every category the policy
  offers, each with a switch, expandable description, and declared vendors,
  plus a footer with Reject All, Accept All, and Save Settings. Necessary
  stays on with a disabled switch.

  - Save records the current switches. Accept All and Reject All record a
    choice for every unlocked category in the policy.
  - Switches start off when an active durable opt-out or Global Privacy
    Control (GPC) signal restricts that category, even when a stored choice
    granted it. Otherwise they start from the recorded choice, or from the
    policy's defaults before any choice (on under an opt-out policy or for
    preselected categories, otherwise off).
  - When a durable opt-out is held, a status line (`role="status"`) appears
    above the category list: "Opt-out request honored" for a visitor request,
    or "Opt-out preference signal honored" when GPC recorded or enforces it.
    While GPC is active under a policy that maps it, signal-restricted switches
    and `marketing` (or `measurement` when `marketing` is not in scope) stay
    off and disabled with a notice below each locked row, and Accept All and
    Save skip locked categories. When an account holds the opt-out and GPC is
    off, the account notice appears below `marketing` (or `measurement` when
    `marketing` is not in scope) and the switches stay usable. Turning
    `measurement` on while `marketing` stays off allows `measurement` without
    lifting the opt-out; turning `marketing` back on and saving asks the backend to lift the account's opt-out, which stays in force until the backend confirms it. Read
    the same derived state with `useOptOutState()`.
  - A choice saved elsewhere updates untouched switches and preserves edits
    the visitor already made.
  - If the policy changes while the page is open, the widget shows "Privacy
    choices have changed." with a reload button and blocks Save until
    reviewed.

  The widget renders nothing until the visitor's policy resolves or when the
  policy requires no consent UI. It does not open or close any other surface.
  `ConsentManager` renders the same widget inside the dialog.
</section>

<section id="props">
  ## Props

  | Prop | Type | Default | Behavior |
  | --- | --- | --- | --- |
  | `hideBranding` | `boolean` | `true` | Hides the "Secured by" tag. |
  | `noStyle` | `boolean` | `false` | Removes built-in classes from every part so your CSS starts from plain markup. |
  | `language` | `string` | The resolved language | Sets text direction (`dir`). Does not change copy. |
</section>

<section id="accessibility">
  ## Accessibility

  Each switch is a `button` with `role="switch"`, `aria-checked`, and the
  category title as its label. Each row expands with a button setting
  `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls`, keeping one row open at a time. An
  honored opt-out renders a status line with `role="status"` above the list,
  and a GPC or account lock notice renders as an `output` element linked to
  its switch with `aria-describedby`. Footer actions are native buttons. Place
  a heading above the widget as shown.
</section>

<section id="styling">
  ## Style the widget

  `ConsentWidget` reads `components.manager` for its root, opt-out status line
  (`optOutStatus`), and footer, `components.accordion` and
  `components['accordion-item']` for rows, `components.switch` for switches,
  and `components['vendor-list']` for vendor rows. See
  [component parts](/docs/customization/slots). `presentation.preferences` sets
  footer `layout`, `primaryActions`, `direction`, and `uiProfile` for both the
  widget and dialog.
</section>

<section id="verify">
  ## Verify

  Open the page with the widget in a private window. Turn **Analytics** (the
  `measurement` category) on and click Save Settings. Reload and confirm the
  switch stays on and measurement scripts load. Turn it off and save to
  confirm the page reloads without them.
</section>
