<section id="behavior">
  ## What ConsentWidget renders

  `ConsentWidget` renders the preference controls inline in page flow for a
  privacy or account settings page. It displays every category the policy
  offers, each with a switch, expandable description, and declared vendors,
  plus a footer with Reject All, Accept All, and Save Settings. Necessary
  stays on with a disabled switch.

  - Save records the current switches. Accept All and Reject All record a
    choice for every unlocked category in the policy.
  - Switches start off when an active durable opt-out or Global Privacy
    Control (GPC) signal restricts that category, even when a stored choice
    granted it. Otherwise they start from the recorded choice, or from the
    policy's defaults before any choice (on under an opt-out policy or for
    preselected categories, otherwise off).
  - Under an `opt-out` policy, a sale-and-sharing block renders first, above
    the category list and outside any accordion item. Before the visitor opts
    out, the block shows the configured opt-out link title, a neutral status
    sentence, and an `Opt out` button that records the opt-out in one click
    with no Save step. Once a durable opt-out is held, the button disappears
    and the block shows the status line (`role="status"`): "Opt-out request
    honored" for a visitor request, or "Opt-out preference signal honored" when
    GPC recorded or enforces it. Below the status line, the block shows the
    receipt sentence, recorded time, and local reference. When the backend
    confirms the refused rights, the heading names them. When GPC is not
    locking the opt-out, an `Opt back in…` link appears below the receipt.
    Under an `opt-in` policy, no block renders.
  - Under an `opt-out` policy while an opt-out is held, opting back in uses a
    two-step confirmation dialog. Clicking `Opt back in…`, clicking Accept All,
    or turning `marketing` back on and clicking Save Settings opens a separate
    modal dialog instead of saving right away (`measurement` decides when
    `marketing` is not in scope). Focus starts on Cancel, both buttons have
    equal prominence, and pressing Escape cancels with nothing saved.
    Confirming runs the pending action and lifts a device opt-out, or asks the
    backend to lift an account opt-out. Under an `opt-in` policy, Accept All
    still lifts a held opt-out in one step.
  - While GPC is active under a policy that maps it, signal-restricted switches
    and `marketing` (or `measurement` when `marketing` is not in scope) stay
    off and disabled with a notice below each locked row, no `Opt back in…`
    link appears, and Accept All and Save skip locked categories. When an
    account holds the opt-out and GPC is off, the account notice appears below
    `marketing` (or `measurement` when `marketing` is not in scope) and the
    switches stay usable. Turning `measurement` on while `marketing` stays off
    allows `measurement` without lifting the opt-out and skips confirmation.
    Turning `marketing` back on and saving, or clicking `Opt back in…`, opens
    confirmation before asking the backend to lift the account's opt-out, which
    stays in force until the backend confirms it. Read the same derived state
    and receipt with `useOptOutState()`.
  - A choice saved elsewhere updates untouched switches and preserves edits
    the visitor already made.
  - If the policy changes while the page is open, the widget shows "Privacy
    choices have changed." with a reload button and blocks Save until
    reviewed.

  The widget renders nothing until the visitor's policy resolves or when the
  policy requires no consent UI. It does not open or close any other surface.
  `ConsentManager` renders the same widget inside the dialog.
</section>

<section id="props">
  ## Props

  | Prop | Type | Default | Behavior |
  | --- | --- | --- | --- |
  | `hideBranding` | `boolean` | `true` | Hides the "Secured by" tag. |
  | `noStyle` | `boolean` | `false` | Removes built-in classes from every part so your CSS starts from plain markup. |
  | `language` | `string` | The resolved language | Sets text direction (`dir`). Does not change copy. |
</section>

<section id="accessibility">
  ## Accessibility

  Each switch is a `button` with `role="switch"`, `aria-checked`, and the
  category title as its label. Each row expands with a button setting
  `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls`, keeping one row open at a time. Under an
  `opt-out` policy, the sale-and-sharing block renders as a `section` labelled
  by its heading above the list, with `role="status"` on the honored status
  line inside it. A GPC or account lock notice renders as an `output` element
  linked to its switch with `aria-describedby`. Footer actions are native
  buttons. Place a heading above the widget as shown.
</section>

<section id="styling">
  ## Style the widget

  `ConsentWidget` reads `components.manager` for its root, sale-and-sharing
  block (`optOut`, theme slot `consentWidgetOptOut`), opt-out status line
  (`optOutStatus`), and footer, `components.accordion` and
  `components['accordion-item']` for rows, `components.switch` for switches,
  and `components['vendor-list']` for vendor rows. See
  [component parts](/docs/customization/slots). `presentation.preferences` sets
  footer `layout`, `primaryActions`, `direction`, and `uiProfile` for both the
  widget and dialog.
</section>

<section id="verify">
  ## Verify

  Open the page with the widget in a private window. Turn **Analytics** (the
  `measurement` category) on and click Save Settings. Reload and confirm the
  switch stays on and measurement scripts load. Turn it off and save to
  confirm the page reloads without them.
</section>
