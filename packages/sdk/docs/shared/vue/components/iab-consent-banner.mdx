<section id="behavior">
  ## What IABConsentBanner renders

  `IABConsentBanner` renders the IAB TCF first layer while `activeUI` is
  `'banner'`, `iab` is enabled, and the visitor's policy uses the `iab` model
  with a vendor list. `InthProvider` renders it automatically in place of
  `ConsentBanner` under an IAB policy, so import it only when composing
  surfaces yourself.

  The banner lists the purposes, stacks, and special features your vendors
  use, with the vendor count as a link. Its footer shows Reject All, Accept
  All, and Customize.

  - Accept All and Reject All save an IAB choice and write the TC String after
    the Global Vendor List finishes loading. If the list fails to load, the
    banner stays open and grants nothing.
  - Customize opens the IAB preference center on the Purposes tab.
  - The vendor count link opens the Vendors tab.

  Copy comes from the resolved IAB translations, and the component ships its
  own styles.
</section>

<section id="props">
  ## Props

  | Prop | Type | Default | Behavior |
  | --- | --- | --- | --- |
  | `primaryButton` | `'reject' \| 'accept' \| 'customize'` | `'customize'` | Sets which action receives the primary button style. |

  Keep Accept All and Reject All at equal prominence unless legal review
  specifies otherwise.
</section>

<section id="accessibility">
  ## Accessibility and styling

  The banner renders in `document.body`. When blocking, it renders as a
  `dialog` with `aria-modal="true"` and traps focus; otherwise it renders as a
  `region`. `components['iab-banner']` binds attributes to parts including
  `root`, `card`, `title`, `purposeList`, `partnersLink`, and `footer`.
  `iabBannerHideBranding` hides the "Secured by" tag, and `iabBannerModels`
  limits which policy models display it.
</section>
