<section id="behavior">
  ## What IABConsentDialog renders

  `IABConsentDialog` renders the IAB TCF preference center while `activeUI` is
  `'manager'`, `iab` is enabled, and the visitor's policy uses the `iab`
  model. `InthProvider` loads it in place of `ConsentManager` under an IAB
  policy.

  The dialog has two tabs. Purposes lists each purpose, stack, and special
  feature with consent and legitimate-interest switches. Vendors lists every
  vendor, including custom vendors, with search and per-vendor switches. The
  footer saves the selection or accepts or rejects everything and writes the
  TC String.

  To build a custom preference center, read and edit selections with
  `useConsentIabSelection()` and persist them with `useConsentIabSave()`.
</section>

<section id="props">
  ## Props

  | Prop | Type | Default | Behavior |
  | --- | --- | --- | --- |
  | `initialTab` | `'purposes' \| 'vendors'` | Tab requested by the banner, else `'purposes'` | Initial active tab when the dialog opens. |
</section>

<section id="accessibility">
  ## Accessibility and styling

  The dialog renders in `document.body` with `role="dialog"` and a close
  button. While blocking, it sets `aria-modal="true"`, locks scrolling, and
  traps focus. `components['iab-dialog']` binds attributes to `root`, `card`,
  `header`, `tabsList`, `tabPanel`, and `footer`.
  `components['iab-purpose-item']`, `components['iab-stack-item']`, and
  `components['iab-vendor-list']` target row parts. `iabDialogHideBranding`
  hides the "Secured by" tag.
</section>
