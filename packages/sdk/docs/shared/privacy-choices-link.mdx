"Your Privacy Choices" and "Your California Privacy Choices" are the titles of
California's alternative opt-out link, and choosing one commits your site to
more than Inth covers. Under 11 CCR §7015(a), "The Alternative Opt-out Link
shall direct the consumer to a webpage that informs them of both their right to
opt-out of sale/sharing and right to limit and provides them with the
opportunity to exercise both rights."

Inth covers the opt-out of sale and sharing. It has nothing for the right to
limit the use of sensitive personal information. If your site is subject to
that right, provide your own way to exercise it where this link leads, or use
the "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" title.
