The `activeCampaignTracking` helper blocks ActiveCampaign until the visitor grants `marketing` consent. Remove any direct tracking snippet or tag-manager copy, and enable site tracking and authorize your domain in ActiveCampaign.

## Options

| Option | Default | Behavior |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `accountId` | Required | ActiveCampaign account ID. Locked once loading starts on this page. |
| `email` | Unset | Contact email for future requests, updated via `setEmail` after consent. Omitting or blanking it clears the runtime email field. |
| `category` | `'marketing'` | Consent condition for the loader. |
| `scriptUrl` | `https://diffuser-cdn.app-us1.com/diffuser/diffuser.js` | Regional or proxied loader URL. Blank values use the default. Locked once loading starts on this page. |

Missing or blank required values log an error and skip loading.

## Loading and revocation

Before consent, Inth creates no globals, loads no script, and starts nothing without a browser `document`.

On grant, Inth creates the callable `pgo` queue, sets its alias, timestamp, account ID, and `setTrackByDefault(false)`, then loads `diffuser.js` and calls `process('allowTracking')` once. Because `allowTracking` sends the initial page request, the helper skips a duplicate `process` call. The default URL loads `diffuser.js` directly rather than `prism.js`, which redirected to `diffuser.js,?` and returned 403 during testing. If you set a regional or proxied `scriptUrl`, verify that it serves the matching ActiveCampaign runtime.

Keep one `accountId`, `scriptUrl`, and `email` across registrations on the page:

- **Shared consent.** Every active registration must pass consent before tracking starts. Conflicting configurations withhold commands, and changing `accountId` or `scriptUrl` after loading starts logs a conflict and keeps the original SDK until reload. Changing `category` updates Inth eligibility only. Updates settle after the current turn.
- **Email updates.** Recreating the helper with a new `email` after sign-in reuses the loaded tag and applies to future requests without sending another page request. Clearing `email` clears the runtime field only, not tracking cookies, the visitor ID, or identity already synced to ActiveCampaign.
- **Revocation limits.** Keep `reloadOnConsentRevoked` enabled. ActiveCampaign has no documented stop command, and `setTrackByDefault(false)` does not stop services started by `allowTracking`. Once tracking starts, any settled denial from an active registration requires a page reload. The helper sends no further commands even after a regrant and leaves the tag in the DOM so remounting cannot install a second runtime.
- **Reload triggers.** Reload manually if you switch a running helper to a denied category, because `reloadOnConsentRevoked` watches consent changes rather than helper replacements, or if another script replaces `pgo`, a download fails, a mount is interrupted, or the tag detaches. A download that completes without an active consenting registration waits until all active registrations agree and pass consent.

ActiveCampaign writes first-party tracking cookies whose names depend on your account configuration. Inspect DevTools Application after granting consent.

## Verify ActiveCampaign

Filter DevTools Network by `diffuser.js`:

1. Before choosing and after granting an unrelated category, confirm no `diffuser.js` request, `pgo`, `prismGlobalObjectAlias`, cookies, or storage appear.
2. Allow `marketing`. Confirm the vendor runtime replaces the `pgo` queue and sends one page request to your configured account.
3. Withdraw `marketing` and confirm the reloaded page does not load the tracker.
