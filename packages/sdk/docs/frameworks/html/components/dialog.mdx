The preference dialog, "Your Privacy Choices", is the full consent manager.
`inth.hosted.js` renders it from `inth.choices.js` without extra markup. It is
centred on desktop and a bottom sheet on phones.

## What the dialog shows

The dialog stays closed until opened by:

- a [`data-inth-action="customize"` button](/docs/frameworks/html/components/action-buttons);
- a [link to `#inth-preferences`](/docs/frameworks/html/components/preferences-link);
- a "Your Privacy Choices" link from `createPrivacyChoicesLink`;
- the [floating trigger](/docs/frameworks/html/components/trigger);
- `inth.openDialog()` in JavaScript.

The banner's **Customize** button, and the opt-out link on a notice, do not
open the dialog. They open the choices inside the banner. Opening the dialog records nothing and changes no
permissions.

## Which categories it lists

`necessary` appears first (always on, locked), followed by optional
categories used on the page within the policy's scope:

- categories referenced on `<script type="text/plain" data-inth-category>`,
  `<iframe data-category>`, `scripts` entries and network blocker rules;
- categories listed in `data-categories` on the tag or `consentCategories` in
  `config`.

If the page declares no categories, the dialog lists every category in the
policy scope. Use `data-categories` when a tag manager or external script
needs a category not present in page tags:

{/* example: fragment */}

```html
<script
  src="https://your-project.inth.app/inth.js"
  data-categories="measurement,marketing"
  defer
></script>
```

See [consent categories](/docs/concepts/consent-categories) and
[vendor consent](/docs/frameworks/html/vendor-consent).

## What it asks

The dialog's content follows the policy regime:

- **Choice regime** (opt-in, and opt-out rules that ask for a choice): one
  switch per category in scope, with its vendors, and **Reject all**,
  **Accept all** and **Save**. Save stays disabled until a switch differs from
  the recorded choice. Closing without saving discards unsaved switches.
- **Opt-out regime** (opt-out rules with a notice): the sale-and-sharing row
  with **Protect** and **Allow**, the receipt of the recorded choice, a
  **Preferences** row for the category switches, and **Done**. A switch there
  applies at once. Allowing again after an opt-out asks to confirm first
  (11 CCR §7028(a)); a choice that grants every category, such as a page's
  `data-inth-action="accept"`, opens the same confirmation as a separate modal
  dialog. **Accept** never lifts a held opt-out.

An opt-out made from a one-click opt-out link, or confirmed after the reload
an opt-out triggers, opens the dialog on its receipt.

While Global Privacy Control is active under a policy that maps it, the
signal-restricted categories stay off, their switches are disabled with the GPC
note, and **Allow** is unavailable. When an account holds the opt-out, lifting
it asks the backend, and a refused request leaves it in place.

The dialog's copy comes from the `privacyChoices` translation section and
`consentTypes.<category>.title` and `.description` for each category.

Setting `ui: { dialog: false }` disables the stock dialog. Use it only when
rendering your own dialog with [headless](/docs/frameworks/html/headless).

## Keyboard and screen readers

- Renders as `role="dialog"` with `aria-modal="true"` and `aria-labelledby`
  pointing at its title. It always blocks the page: backdrop, scroll lock and
  focus trap.
- Escape and **Close** close it without saving and restore the banner if a
  choice is still due. Clicking the backdrop does not close it. Focus returns
  to the element that opened it, else to a "Your Privacy Choices" link, else
  to the trigger.
- Each category switch is a `role="switch"` named by its category title. The
  `necessary` switch is disabled and always on.
- On phones, the Preferences row and each category's vendor list open as a
  nested sheet with its own title and close button.
- Sets `lang` and `dir` from the resolved language.

## Style it

Shared theme tokens apply automatically (see
[customize](/docs/frameworks/html/customize)). Target specific parts in
`ui.css` by `data-testid`:

| `data-testid` | Element |
| --- | --- |
| `consent-dialog-root` | Backdrop and root, carrying `data-regime` (`eu` or `us`) and `data-layout` (`desktop` or `phone`). |
| `consent-dialog-card`, `consent-dialog-header`, `consent-dialog-content`, `consent-dialog-footer` | Card and its sections. |
| `consent-dialog-title`, `consent-dialog-intro`, `consent-dialog-close` | Heading, introduction and close button. |
| `consent-dialog-right-option-sale-protect`, `consent-dialog-right-option-sale-allow` | The sale-and-sharing choice. |
| `consent-dialog-receipt`, `consent-dialog-receipt-toggle`, `consent-dialog-receipt-details` | The receipt of the recorded choice. |
| `consent-dialog-preferences-row`, `consent-dialog-preferences-panel` | The Preferences row and its categories. |
| `consent-dialog-category-switch-<category>` | Category switch. |
| `consent-dialog-reject-all`, `consent-dialog-accept-all`, `consent-dialog-save-button`, `consent-dialog-done` | Action buttons. |
| `consent-dialog-sheet`, `consent-dialog-sheet-close` | A nested sheet on phones. |
| `opt-in-confirmation-root`, `opt-in-confirmation-card`, `opt-in-confirmation-title`, `opt-in-confirmation-description` | Separate modal confirmation dialog for opting back in under an `opt-out` policy. |
| `opt-in-confirmation-cancel-button`, `opt-in-confirmation-confirm-button` | Equal-prominence cancel ("Stay opted out") and confirm ("Opt back in") buttons. |

## Check it works

1. Use an opt-in policy. Click a **Privacy settings** link. A centred card
   opens over a dimmed backdrop, with focus on its title.
2. Turn on **Analytics** (`measurement`) only and click **Save**.
   Measurement vendors load while marketing stays blocked.
3. Reopen the dialog and confirm **Analytics** is on and **Marketing** is
   off.
4. Toggle **Marketing** on, press Escape and reopen. **Marketing** remains
   off.
