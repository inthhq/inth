Turn any button or link in your Angular app into a control that reopens the
preference dialog with `inthConsentDialogLink`.

## Add a preferences link

Import `ConsentDialogLink` and add `inthConsentDialogLink` to your own
`<button>` or `<a>`, typically in the footer:

<include src="../../../shared/examples/angular/app-component.mdx" />

Clicking the element sets `activeUI` to `'dialog'`, opening
`<inth-consent-dialog>`. Keep the dialog rendered in your root component. A
click handler that runs first and calls `event.preventDefault()` cancels the
open.

The directive adds no classes or markup. Your element keeps its own styling
and label, so translate its text with the rest of your page.

## Behavior

- When the policy offers no preference management
  (`!ConsentManager.hasConsentPreferences()`), the directive sets `hidden` on
  the host and removes its test ID.
- When visible, the host carries `data-testid="consent-dialog-link"` and
  `data-inth-rights` with the rights granted by the policy.
- It prefetches the deferred dialog chunk during idle time and on hover or
  focus.

On an `<a>`, default navigation is prevented. Prefer `<button>` unless your
design requires a link.

## Verify it

Accept or reject in the banner, then click the link. The preference dialog
opens showing your saved switches. Under a policy with no preferences, the
element stays hidden.
