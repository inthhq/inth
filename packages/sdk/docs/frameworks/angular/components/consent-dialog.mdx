Render the "Your Privacy Choices" dialog, where visitors review and change
their consent, with `<inth-consent-dialog>`.

## Render the dialog

Import `ConsentDialog` from `@inth/angular/consent-dialog` and render
`<inth-consent-dialog>` once in your root component, next to
`<inth-consent-banner>`:

<include src="../../../shared/examples/angular/app-component.mdx" />

Do not wrap it in `@if`: the dialog stays closed until something opens it.
Use [`<inth-provider>`](./inth-provider) to render banner and dialog in one
tag.

## Open the dialog

The dialog opens when `activeUI()` becomes `'dialog'`. These set it:

- [`<inth-privacy-choices-link>`](./privacy-choices-link),
  [`[inthConsentDialogLink]`](./consent-dialog-link) and
  [`<inth-consent-dialog-trigger>`](./consent-dialog-trigger).
- The button inside an `<inth-consent-gate>` placeholder.
- `ConsentManager.setActiveUI('dialog')` or `HeadlessConsent.openDialog()` in
  your own code.

The banner's Customize button does not open the dialog. It opens the choices
inside the banner, so a first-time visitor never leaves the banner to choose.
The dialog is where visitors come back later, which is why a footer link to
it matters.

## What the dialog contains

The dialog is centred on desktop and a bottom sheet on phones. Its content
follows the policy:

- **Choice regime** (opt-in, and opt-out rules that ask for a choice): one
  switch per category in scope, with its vendors, and Reject All, Accept All
  and Save. Save stays disabled until a switch differs from the recorded
  choice, so a visitor cannot save the same choice by accident.
- **Opt-out regime** (opt-out rules with a notice): the sale-and-sharing row
  with Protect and Allow, the receipt of the recorded choice, a Preferences
  row for the category switches, and Done. Allowing again after an opt-out
  asks to confirm first (11 CCR §7028(a)).

On phones, Preferences and each category's vendor list open as nested sheets
over the dialog.

While Global Privacy Control is active under a policy that maps it, the
signal-restricted categories stay off and their switches are disabled with
the GPC note. Read the same state and receipt from
`ConsentManager.optOutState()`.

After a one-click opt-out, from a "Do Not Sell" link or
`ConsentManager.requestOptOut()`, the dialog opens on its receipt with focus
there. A standalone `<inth-opt-out-confirmation>` you mounted still shows
itself instead.

## Inputs

| Input | Type | Default | Behavior |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `open` | `boolean` | follows `activeUI()` | Controlled visibility. See [control the open state](#control-the-open-state). |
| `showTrigger` | `boolean` or `ConsentDialogTriggerOptions` | `false` | Renders a floating `<inth-consent-dialog-trigger>` with the dialog. |
| `legalLinks` | array of `LegalLinks` keys, or `null` | shown | The footer shows the Cookie Policy under a choice regime and the Privacy Policy under an opt-out regime. `null` hides it; an array shows it only when its key is listed. |
| `models` | `Model[]` | `['opt-in', 'opt-out', 'none']` | Policy models handled. IAB uses `<inth-iab-consent-dialog>`. |
| `noStyle` | `boolean` | provider `noStyle` | Drops `inth` classes. |
| `disableAnimation` | `boolean` | provider value | Shows the backdrop, card and phone sheets without motion. |

Copy comes from the `privacyChoices` translation section and
`consentTypes.<category>.title` and `.description` for each category; see
[translations](../translations). Passing `showTrigger` loads the dialog chunk
on mount. Render `<inth-consent-dialog-trigger>` on its own to avoid that
eager load.

## Close the dialog

Escape, Close and Done close the dialog without saving. If the policy still owes a
choice, the banner returns. Clicking the backdrop does not close it, so
unsaved switches are not lost to a stray click. Closing discards unsaved
switches, so the next open starts from the recorded choice.

Reject All, Accept All and Save close the dialog as soon as the browser
records the choice. The request to Inth runs afterwards and never reopens the
dialog: a failed request keeps the local choice, reports through `onError`
and retries later.

The dialog always blocks the page: backdrop, scroll lock and focus trap.

## Control the open state

Bind `[(open)]` when your own state should decide whether the dialog shows:

{/* example: fragment */}

```html
<inth-consent-dialog [(open)]="preferencesOpen" />
```

`true` asks the consent manager once to show the dialog, so the banner hands
over and phone sheets, Escape and the receipt work as for any open dialog.
Close, Done, Escape and any choice close it through the consent manager and
emit `openChange` with `false`. To ask again,
`open` must turn `false` and back to `true`. `false` hides the dialog whatever
the consent manager shows; turning it `false` while the dialog shows closes it
as Close does, so an unanswered banner comes back.

## How the dialog loads

`ConsentDialog` is a small host. The dialog body is a chunk of its own,
with its stylesheet, so it stays out of the first page load. It loads at the
first of these:

- Browser idle time after `load`, while a control that opens the dialog is
  rendered: `<inth-privacy-choices-link>`, `[inthConsentDialogLink]`,
  `<inth-consent-dialog-trigger>`, an `<inth-consent-gate>` placeholder or a
  `ConsentButton` with `action="customize"`.
- Hover or focus on any of those controls.
- Opening the dialog.

Set `preloadDialog: 'intent'` in `provideInth()` to skip idle loading and load
only on hover, focus or open. Idle loading also skips on Save-Data, 2G and
offline connections. If loading fails while opening, the previous surface
returns (or `openChange` emits `false` for a controlled dialog) and the next
open tries again.

The dialog stylesheet loads with the chunk as a `<style>` element stamped
with the CSP nonce. With `styles: false`, the rules come from
`@inth/angular/styles.css`.

The dialog never opens without a resolved policy rule, even when
`activeUI()` is already `'dialog'`. It appears as soon as a rule resolves. A
rule with `model: 'none'` and no rights owes no consent UI, so the dialog
stays closed. When a `none` rule lists a right, the dialog opens as a
settings route and closing it writes no consent record.

## Accessibility

- The dialog carries `role="dialog"`, `aria-modal="true"` and
  `aria-labelledby` pointing at its title, and sets `dir` from the active
  language.
- Opening moves focus inside the dialog. Tab and Shift+Tab wrap inside it.
  Closing returns focus to the control that opened it, or to "Your Privacy
  Choices" when that control is gone.
- Each category switch uses the category title as its accessible name. The
  `necessary` switch is disabled and always on.
- Each nested phone sheet has its own title and close button.
- Status messages, such as the saved cue, use a `role="status"` region.

## Style the dialog

`<inth-consent-dialog>` reads these `theme.slots` keys: `consentDialog` (the
backdrop, which is the dialog's root), `consentDialogCard`,
`consentDialogSheet`, `consentDialogHeader`, `consentDialogTitle`,
`consentDialogContent`, `consentDialogFooter`, `consentDialogCategoryGroup`,
`consentDialogVendorTable`, `consentDialogRightsRow`, `consentDialogReceipt`,
`consentDialogPreferencesRow`, `consentDialogSaveButton` and
`consentDialogDoneButton`. See [customize](../customize).

The root carries `data-testid="consent-dialog-root"`, `data-regime` (`eu` or
`us`) and `data-layout` (`desktop` or `phone`). For your own dialog markup,
use [`<inth-consent-widget>`](./consent-widget) or the
[headless](../headless) state.

## Verify it

Test with an opt-in policy and an optional category not restricted by policy
or GPC. Open the dialog from `<inth-privacy-choices-link>`. Confirm a centred
card opens over a dimmed backdrop, background scroll locks, and Tab stays
inside the card. Press Escape and confirm the dialog closes and focus returns
to the link. Reopen it, turn on a category and click Save. Confirm the dialog
closes and `ConsentManager.has('<category>')` returns `true`. Reload and
reopen the dialog to confirm the switch keeps the saved choice.
