### Write the IAB GPP string for US National and TCF EU

Swift and Android can now write an IAB Global Privacy Platform string. Turn it
on with `CoreConfig(gpp: GPPOptions())` or `C15tAppleConfiguration.gpp` on
Swift, `NativeConfig(gpp = GppOptions())` on Kotlin, or the `com.c15t.gpp`
`Info.plist` key and `com.c15t.GPP` Android metadata. GPP stays off until you
do.

- A US visitor under a rule that offers an opt-out gets the US National
  section, MSPA version 2. Refusing `marketing`, or the categories in
  `optOutCategories`, reports an opt-out of sale, sharing and targeted
  advertising. Sensitive data and known-child consents are reported as not
  applicable.
- An `iab` rule gets the TCF EU section once the visitor saves an IAB choice:
  the committed TC String, unchanged.
- The string is on `snapshot.gppString`. The engine writes the `IABGPP_HDR_*`,
  `IABGPP_GppSID`, `IABGPP_[ID]_String` and `IABGPP_TCFEU2_*` keys next to the
  `IABTCF_*` keys, and removes them when no section applies.
- `mspaMode` reports a covered transaction for MSPA signatories, and
  `tcf: false` leaves the TCF EU section out.

The cores now keep the matched rule's `rights` from `/init`.
